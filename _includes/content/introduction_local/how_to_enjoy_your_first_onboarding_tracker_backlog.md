
### What?
The local onboarding track is a hands-on introduction to some of the systems you'll be working on/with during your time as a Tanzu CF engineer.

Even if you're not an engineer, if you're curious about the essentials of the CF or Concourse user experience, then this onboarding track is for you.

### How?
1. Take your time.
1. Read the **[troubleshooting]({{ 'troubleshooting' | relative_url }})** doc
1. If something is missing / is wrong / could be improved, create an issue in the **[pivotal/cf-onboarding repo](https://github.com/pivotal/cf-onboarding/issues)**.
1. To begin, click on the next topic in the menu to the left.
